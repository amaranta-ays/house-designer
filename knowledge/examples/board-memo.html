Re
Q3 operating margin compression

Margin compression returned in Q3.

Operating margin slipped 180 basis points in Q3 as input costs outpaced pricing actions across the wholesale segment. Pricing taken in July partially offset the headwind; the lag is meaningful and likely to extend into Q4.

Analysis

Three categories accounted for nearly all of the deterioration: freight (60bps), packaging (50bps), and contract labor (45bps). Pricing actions taken in July partially offset the headwind, but the lag is meaningful and likely to extend into Q4. The wholesale segment carried two-thirds of the variance; retail and direct channels held within tolerance.

Recommendation

Accelerate the second pricing pass to Q4 from Q1 2027. Expand the contract-labor sourcing pilot to two additional sites. Monitor freight carrier consolidation closely; renegotiate Q1 contracts ahead of schedule.
